Solar tilt control for Home Assistant · v0.1.0 stable

Follow the sun.
Stow before the storm.

Helios Tilt works out where the sun is, picks the best angle for each panel and drives your linear actuator there. When the wind picks up, it stows the panels first.

What you get

One device per panel, set up from the UI. Everything runs locally: no cloud service and no extra Python packages.

Real sun position

The NOAA algorithm with refraction, checked against reference values, and a clear-sky model to weigh the angles.

Four strategies

Fixed annual optimum, a seasonal curve, one move a day to the day's optimum, or full tracking with a cloudy-day mode and night park.

Wind first

Stows on wind or gusts from your weather entity and anemometer, in any unit. Resumes after a calm period; storm mode latches for hours.

Any actuator

A cover, a number, a script, or a dumb actuator on two relays (such as a Tuya ZG-2002-RF): timed runs with an interlock, a countdown fail-safe, guided travel calibration and automatic homing. Linear or calibration-table mapping and position or inclinometer feedback.

Your rules

Your own rain, snow and temperature sensors, custom stow triggers, quiet hours (the night park still happens), a timed stow and an optional wind direction rule. Attributes show which source drives each decision.

Gentle on hardware

Never commands a moving actuator. Minimum interval, deadband, hysteresis, a pause after manual moves, and a repair notice when an actuator gets stuck.

Dashboard card

A side view with the sun, a wind gauge, Auto/Stow/Manual buttons, an angle slider, today's plan and the last moves. Loaded automatically.

Works with dumb actuators

Two cheap relays are enough. Tested on a real Tuya ZG-2002-RF dual relay (Zigbee2MQTT) driving an attic window opener.

  • Time-based position with separate open and close times.
  • Homing against the actuator's limit switches: first move, after N moves, before the night park, or with Home now.
  • Interlock: the other relay is switched off and confirmed first; both-on is caught.
  • Countdown fail-safe: the relay switches itself off even if Home Assistant crashes mid-run.
  • RF remote use is detected and followed; automatic control pauses.
  • Relay offline: nothing is switched, a repair appears, and it recovers by itself.

Time the travel (required)

  1. Enter the panel angle when fully closed and fully open.
  2. Panel ⋮ → Reconfigure → Time the travel.
  3. Submit when it stops fully open, then again when it stops fully closed.
  4. Save, press Home now, test a wind stow.

Zigbee2MQTT: power_on_behavior off on both channels; inching off, or longer than full travel × 1.15 + 5 s. Fuse the supply. Full guide and wiring.

How the angle is chosen

Every minute, and whenever the weather or an actuator changes, the first rule that applies wins.

  1. Storm or wind stow: stow angle, immediately
  2. Custom stow trigger: trigger angle, immediately
  3. Stowed by hand (optionally for a duration): stow angle
  4. No valid wind data: hold (stay stowed if it was windy)
  5. Snow / heavy rain / frost: their own angles (optional)
  6. Manual mode: your angle
  7. Paused after a move by hand, or automatic control off: hold
  8. Quiet hours: hold (the night park still happens)
  9. Strategy: rounded to the step, with hysteresis, a deadband and a minimum interval

Why the maximum angle all autumn and winter? At 52° N the best daily angle is about 58° in October and 75° at Christmas, steeper than most mounts allow, so every strategy clamps to your maximum. Prefer a weather entity with gusts (e.g. Buienradar).

Mechanical safety

Helios Tilt is software. It can't see your mount, and Home Assistant can go offline at the wrong moment.

  • Use actuators with hardware end stops or limit switches, and fuse their supply.
  • Set the soft limits inside the mechanical range and test the stow angle for your mount and local wind loads.
  • For storm protection that never depends on Home Assistant, add a local fallback in the actuator controller.
  • Keep people and pets clear of moving mounts. MIT licence, no warranty.

Installation

HACS, a restart, and a weather entity or wind sensor.

HACS

  1. 1. Click Open in HACS and download Helios Tilt.
  2. 2. Restart Home Assistant.
  3. 3. Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Helios Tilt.
  4. 4. Pick your weather entity, then the first panel's actuator, azimuth, limits and strategy. Two relays: time the travel. To add more panels, use ⋮ → Add panel.
